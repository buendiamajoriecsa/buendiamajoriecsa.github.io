Project 2: Campus WiFi Performance Analysis

Data Analytics & Spreadsheet Case Study

Scenario: Evaluated the performance of the campus WiFi network using Microsoft Excel. Analyzed 50 WiFi records to identify network performance patterns, user types, location bottlenecks, and provided evidence-based recommendations[cite: 11, 15].

1. Data Inspection & Descriptive Statistics:

  • Data Cleaning: Checked for missing/invalid values and duplicate record IDs (None observed in the 50 records)[cite: 11]. Ensured numerical formatting for speeds, connected devices, and downtime[cite: 11].
  • Download Speed: Mean = 76.51 Mbps | Median = 77.10 Mbps | Min = 44.5 Mbps | Max = 103.8 Mbps[cite: 11]
  • Upload Speed: Mean = 21.91 Mbps | Median = 20.95 Mbps | Min = 9.10 Mbps | Max = 34.5 Mbps[cite: 11]
  • Connected Devices: Mean = 40.28 | Median = 47 | Min = 8 | Max = 64[cite: 11]
  • Downtime Minutes: Mean = 18.40 | Median = 18.5 | Min = 0 | Max = 45[cite: 11]
  • Satisfaction Rating: Mean = 4.22 | Median = 4.25 | Min = 2.80 | Max = 5.0 (Out of 5)[cite: 11]

2. User Types & Campus Location Insights:

  • User Distribution (Total 50): Students = 37 | Faculty = 9 | Staff = 4[cite: 12]
  • Highest Average Download Speed: Computer Laboratory (82.44 Mbps)[cite: 12, 14]
  • Lowest Average Download Speed: Student Center (65.63 Mbps)[cite: 12, 14]
  • Highest Average Downtime: Student Center (28.20 minutes)[cite: 12, 14]

3. Correlation Analysis:

  • Connected Devices vs. Download Speed: $r = -0.009$ (Almost no relationship)[cite: 12, 14].
  • Downtime vs. Satisfaction Rating: $r = -0.748$ (Strong negative correlation, indicating that higher downtime leads to lower user satisfaction)[cite: 12, 13].

4. Evidence-Based Recommendations:

  1. Investigate the Student Center: Address the lowest download speed (65.63 Mbps) and highest downtime (28.20 mins) by upgrading equipment and network capacity[cite: 12, 14].
  2. Reduce Network Downtime: Conduct regular network monitoring and maintenance to minimize service interruptions and improve user satisfaction[cite: 14].
  3. Continuous Monitoring: Assess network performance based on multiple parameters rather than just connected device counts[cite: 14].
